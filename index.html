FAQ

Questions investors ask

Short answers to the questions I hear most. For anything specific to your situation, book a consultation.

Can I invest in Dubai property from abroad?

Yes. Foreign buyers can purchase in Dubai's designated freehold areas without living in the UAE, and much of the process can be handled remotely.

What does off plan mean?

You buy a property before or during construction and pay in stages, usually a deposit, instalments during the build and the balance at handover.

How much do I need to start?

It depends on the project and its payment plan. In a private consultation we map options to your exact budget and goals.

Can property in Dubai lead to a Golden Visa?

Owning property with a Dubai Land Department certified value of at least AED 2 million can make you eligible to apply for a renewable 10 year UAE Golden Visa.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first private consultation is free and there is no obligation.
