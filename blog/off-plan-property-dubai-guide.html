Off plan property in Dubai: how it works for international investors

By Ilinka, Real Estate Consultant at Capital Solutions Group
16 October 2026 · 7 minute read
Short answer

Buying off plan means you reserve a property before or during construction and pay in stages, usually with a deposit at booking, instalments during the build and a final payment at handover. In Dubai, payments for registered off plan projects go into a project escrow account, and the sale is recorded with the Dubai Land Department through the Oqood system.

What off plan means

An off plan property is one you buy from the developer before it is finished, sometimes before construction has started. You choose a unit from plans, renders and a show apartment, sign a sale and purchase agreement and pay in stages until the building is handed over.

International investors choose off plan for three main reasons: a lower amount of capital needed at the start, the chance to buy at launch pricing, and a brand new property at handover. The trade off is time. Your money is committed for the length of the build, and you cannot rent the unit until it is complete.

How payment plans work

Every project sets its own schedule, but most follow the same shape. A typical plan looks like this:

StageWhenWhat you pay
BookingWhen you reserveA deposit, often 10% to 20% of the price
ConstructionDuring the buildInstalments linked to dates or to construction milestones
HandoverOn completionThe remaining balance
Post handover (some projects)After you receive the keysPart of the balance spread over an agreed period

The percentages above are an illustration of a common structure, not an offer. Every developer publishes its own plan, and the version in your signed agreement is the one that counts.

How buyers are protected

Dubai has specific rules for off plan sales that are worth understanding before you commit:

  • Escrow accounts. Under Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007, money paid by buyers for an off plan project goes into an escrow account dedicated to that project, and is released to the developer as construction progresses.
  • Oqood registration. Off plan sales are provisionally registered with the Dubai Land Department (DLD) through the Oqood system, which records you as the buyer of the unit before the title deed exists.
  • RERA oversight. The Real Estate Regulatory Agency licenses developers and brokers and oversees projects in Dubai.

Protection rules reduce risk, they do not remove it. Construction can be delayed, and market prices at handover can be higher or lower than your purchase price.

Fees to budget for

The largest cost on top of the price is the DLD registration fee, which is 4% of the property value. Expect smaller fixed administration fees on top. Some developers run promotions that cover part of the DLD fee, so always ask. Once the property is handed over, you will also pay yearly service charges for the building, which affect your net rental yield.

Questions to ask before you reserve

  1. Is the project registered with the DLD, and does it have a dedicated escrow account?
  2. What is the developer's record on handing over previous projects on time?
  3. What is the full payment schedule, and what happens if a payment is late?
  4. What are the expected service charges per square foot once the building is complete?
  5. What rents are similar finished units in the same community achieving today?
  6. Can the unit be resold before handover, and what conditions apply?

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy off plan property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign buyers can purchase off plan property in Dubai's designated freehold areas, whether or not they live in the UAE.

Is my off plan payment protected in Dubai?

Payments for registered off plan projects go into a project escrow account under Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007 and are released to the developer as construction progresses. This reduces risk but does not remove it.

What fees do I pay when buying off plan in Dubai?

The main fee is the Dubai Land Department registration fee of 4% of the property value, plus smaller administration fees. Service charges apply once the property is handed over.
