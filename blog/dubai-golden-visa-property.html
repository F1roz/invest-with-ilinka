Dubai Golden Visa through property: what investors need to know

By Ilinka, Real Estate Consultant at Capital Solutions Group
16 October 2026 · 5 minute read
Short answer

In Dubai, property owners can apply for a renewable 10 year UAE Golden Visa when the property they own is worth at least AED 2 million, based on its Dubai Land Department certified value. The value can come from one property or several, and off plan and mortgaged properties can qualify under conditions.

The AED 2 million threshold

The property route to the Golden Visa in Dubai is based on the total value of the property you own, as certified by the Dubai Land Department (DLD). The minimum is AED 2 million. That value can be one home or a portfolio of properties registered in your name. The visa is valid for 10 years and can be renewed.

Off plan and mortgaged properties

  • Off plan. Off plan properties can count toward the threshold, based on the DLD certified value of the property registered in your name.
  • Mortgaged. Since January 2024, mortgaged properties can qualify based on their full certified value. You will need a letter from your bank confirming the mortgage.

Joint ownership

If you own a property with someone else, your own share must be worth at least AED 2 million. A property worth AED 3 million owned equally by two people would give each owner a share of AED 1.5 million, which is below the threshold for each of them.

Documents and steps

  1. Confirm the DLD certified value of the property or properties in your name.
  2. Prepare your title deeds (or Oqood registration for off plan), passport and photograph.
  3. If the property is mortgaged, get the confirmation letter from your bank.
  4. Apply through the Dubai Land Department and the residency authorities, then complete the medical test and Emirates ID steps.

Golden Visa holders can sponsor family members for residency. Fees and the exact list of documents change from time to time, so check the latest requirements before applying.

Before you apply

Selling the property can affect renewal, so plan your holding period with the visa in mind. If residency is one of your goals, it helps to shortlist properties that reach the threshold on their own, so you are not relying on a combination of smaller units.

This article is general information, not legal or immigration advice. Confirm current rules and fees with the Dubai Land Department and the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

How much property do I need to buy for a Dubai Golden Visa?

Property with a Dubai Land Department certified value of at least AED 2 million, owned in one property or across several.

Does an off plan property qualify for the Golden Visa?

Yes, off plan property can qualify based on the DLD certified value of the property registered in your name.

Can I get a Golden Visa with a mortgaged property?

Yes. Since January 2024 mortgaged property can qualify based on its full certified value, with a letter from your bank.
