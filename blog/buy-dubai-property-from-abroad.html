How to buy property in Dubai from abroad, step by step

By Ilinka, Real Estate Consultant at Capital Solutions Group
16 October 2026 · 6 minute read
Short answer

Non residents can buy property in Dubai's designated freehold areas with a valid passport. The main steps are: set your goals and budget, shortlist properties, check the seller or developer, sign the sale agreement, pay the deposit and fees, and register the sale with the Dubai Land Department. Much of this can be done remotely, with a power of attorney if someone signs for you.

Where foreigners can buy

Under Dubai Law No. 7 of 2006, foreign buyers can own property in areas designated for foreign ownership, known as freehold areas. Freehold ownership covers the property and the land and is not limited in time. Freehold areas include Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Business Bay, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Village and Arabian Ranches, among others. You do not need to live in the UAE to buy.

The steps

  1. Set your goals. Decide whether you want rental income, capital growth, residency or a home for yourself. This shapes everything else.
  2. Shortlist properties. Compare locations, building quality, payment plans and net yield.
  3. Check the seller or developer. Confirm that the seller owns the property and that it has no outstanding mortgage, or that the developer and project are registered.
  4. Sign the sale agreement. For resale, this is the standard contract issued by the Dubai Land Department. For off plan, it is the developer's sale and purchase agreement.
  5. Pay the deposit and fees. Payments go through traceable bank transfers or cheques, never cash.
  6. Register the sale. The transfer is registered with the Dubai Land Department at a registration trustee office, or through Oqood for off plan.

Documents you will need

  • A valid passport (Emirates ID if you are a UAE resident)
  • The signed sale agreement
  • A no objection certificate from the developer, for resale properties in freehold communities
  • A power of attorney, if someone is signing on your behalf
  • Company documents, if you are buying through a company

Fees to plan for

FeeTypical amount
Dubai Land Department fee4% of the property value
Registration trustee feeAED 2,000 or 4,000 plus VAT, depending on the price
Title deed and admin feesSmaller fixed amounts
Agency commission (resale)Agreed with your agent

Fees change over time and some developers cover part of them on new launches. Confirm the full list for your purchase before you sign.

Buying without flying in

Many international investors complete their purchase without visiting. Viewings can be done by video, contracts can be signed digitally or through a power of attorney, and payments are made by international transfer. For off plan purchases, the developer usually handles the Oqood registration. Visiting at least once before handover is still worthwhile if you can.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy property in Dubai without a residency visa?

Yes. Non residents can buy in Dubai's freehold areas using a valid passport. Residency is not required to own property.

Can I buy Dubai property without visiting?

Yes. Viewings can be done by video, documents signed digitally or through a power of attorney, and payments made by international bank transfer.

What is the Dubai Land Department fee?

The DLD fee is 4% of the property value, plus smaller administration and registration trustee fees.
